THE FFD EXPERIENCE / PREVIEW

Understand it before you choose it.

A proposed visit is designed to help you compare less-lethal options and ask direct questions. The range portion will be offered only after the location, insurance, procedures, and customer access are approved.

HOW IT WOULD WORK

A calm, useful walkthrough.

01

Tell us what matters

Talk through your questions, experience, and what you need a product to do. You do not need to arrive with a model picked out.

02

Compare the options

See relevant products, discuss differences, and cover projectiles, CO₂, accessories, maintenance, storage, and real limitations.

03

See a supervised demo

If the approved process allows it and the range is available, a supervised demonstration would show the product in use. Customer participation and safety steps remain to be defined.

04

Decide at your pace

Leave with a clearer sense of fit, ongoing needs, and questions to research. There is no purchase requirement in this preview concept.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Good questions welcome.

Ask what a launcher can and cannot do, what each projectile is intended for, what it costs to keep a setup ready, and how to store and maintain it responsibly.

Important status

The planned demo location is an Armory range beside the proposed FFD room. Written range access, approved products and projectiles, customer eligibility, supervision, waivers, insurance, and scheduling rules remain open. This page does not promise range access or a customer trial.
